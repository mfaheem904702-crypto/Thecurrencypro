Fact Checking Policy

How we maintain financial accuracy, verify data sources, and conduct daily market audits.

Financial accuracy is vital for our users. We maintain a strict fact-checking protocol to ensure that all numbers, exchange rates, and guides remain reliable.

1. Primary Reference Sources

When reporting economic news or currency trends, we prioritize official reports from reputable sources including:

  • State Bank of Pakistan (SBP)
  • International Monetary Fund (IMF)
  • Federal Board of Revenue (FBR)
  • All Pakistan Sarafa Gems and Jewellers Association

2. Daily Rate Audits

Our system continuously updates interbank exchange rates. Additionally, manual spot-checks are conducted daily to compare open market dealer rates with published rates.
