📌 Understanding Gold Purity & Price Disparities

In South Asian bullion markets—particularly in Pakistan—gold is prized both as a cultural cornerstone for wedding jewelry and as an essential hedge against currency devaluation and inflation. However, many buyers and investors remain confused by the price disparity between 24K pure gold and 22K jewelry gold.

Understanding how purity affects valuations, making charges, and scrap buyback rates is critical to avoid overpaying at local Sarafa bazaars.

1. What Is Karatage? (24K vs 22K Purity Defined)

  • 24K Gold (99.9% Pure): Contains 24 parts pure gold out of 24. It is extremely soft and yellow, primarily used for bullion bars and investment coins.
  • 22K Gold (91.67% Pure): Contains 22 parts gold and 2 parts alloy metals (copper, silver, or zinc) to provide structural hardness required for crafting durable jewelry.

2. Mathematical Formula to Calculate 22K Rate

You can easily calculate the 22K price using the baseline 24K rate quoted by the local jeweler association:

The Gold Purity Multiplier Formula

22K Rate = 24K Base Rate × (22 / 24) = 24K Base Rate × 0.9167

Example: If 24K Gold is quoted at PKR 280,000 / Tola, the baseline price of 22K Gold is:

280,000 × 0.9167 = PKR 256,676 per Tola
