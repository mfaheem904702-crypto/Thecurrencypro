What moves GBP to PKR

Bank of England

Interest-rate decisions and UK inflation data move the pound against the dollar.

UK economy

Jobs, growth and political news change global demand for sterling.

Dollar to rupee

Pakistan's reserves, imports and remittances move the rupee. See USD to PKR.

Remittance flows

Large transfers from the UK add foreign currency to Pakistan's market, especially around festivals.

Like the euro, the pound floats, so GBP to PKR ≈ GBP to USD × USD to PKR.

Sending money from the UK: a checklist

  1. Get the exact rate and fee in writing or on screen before you pay.
  2. Multiply the amount after fees by the rate to see what your family receives.
  3. Compare at least two providers on the same day.
  4. Check the provider is authorised in the UK and the delivery method suits your family: bank account, wallet or cash pickup.
  5. Keep the receipt and tracking number.

Frequently asked questions

What is 1 GBP to PKR today?

The figure at the top of this page is the latest reference rate we fetched for one British pound in Pakistani rupees. Money transfer providers and banks quote their own rates, so confirm before you send.

Why does GBP to PKR change?

It follows the pound-dollar rate and the dollar-rupee rate. UK inflation and Bank of England decisions move the pound, while Pakistan's reserves, imports and remittances move the rupee.

How do I get the best rate sending money from the UK?

Compare the rupees your family receives from two or three providers on the same day, after fees. A better headline rate can still deliver less once fees are added.

Is the pound a safe way to send money home?

Choose a provider authorised by the UK regulator, the FCA, and send through official channels. Keep receipts and check the delivery method before you pay.

Is the rate here the same as my bank or transfer app?

Usually not. This is a reference rate and providers add their own margin and fees.

How often is the rate updated?

The page fetches the latest figure from open.er-api.com each time it loads. The provider updates on its own schedule, shown as Source update, so treat it as a reference rate.
