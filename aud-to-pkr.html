Two moving parts behind AUD to PKR

The Australian dollar floats, so its price in rupees combines the Australian-US dollar rate with the dollar-rupee rate.

AUD to PKR≈AUD to USD×USD to PKR

Commodity prices and Reserve Bank decisions can lift AUD to PKR even if the rupee is steady. Follow the rupee side on USD to PKR.

What moves AUD to PKR

Reserve Bank of Australia

Interest-rate decisions and inflation data move the Australian dollar.

Commodity prices

Australia exports resources such as iron ore, coal and gas, so commodity swings can lift or weigh on its currency.

China demand

China is a major buyer of Australian exports, so Chinese growth news matters.

Dollar to rupee

Pakistan's reserves, imports and remittances move the rupee.

Frequently asked questions

What is 1 AUD to PKR today?

The figure at the top of this page is the latest reference rate we fetched for one Australian Dollar in Pakistani rupees. Banks and exchange companies quote their own rates, so confirm before you transact.

Why does AUD to PKR move with commodity prices?

Australia is a major commodity exporter, so rising or falling resource prices tend to move the Australian dollar, which feeds into AUD to PKR.

Is the Australian dollar pegged?

No. It floats, so AUD to PKR moves with both the Australian-US dollar rate and the dollar-rupee rate.

How do I get the best rate when paying tuition?

Compare the final amount from two or three providers, include all fees, and confirm the rate before you pay.

Is the rate here the same as my bank or exchange company?

Usually not. This is a reference rate and providers add their own margin and fees. Compare the final rupee amount.
