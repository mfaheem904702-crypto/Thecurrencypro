📊 Why Tracking AED, SAR, and EUR Matters in Pakistan

While the US Dollar sets the primary benchmark, the Middle Eastern currencies (Saudi Riyal and UAE Dirham) form the bedrock of overseas worker remittances sent back to families in Pakistan. Meanwhile, the Euro remains critical for European trade settlements and expatriate professionals.

🇸🇦 SAR & AED Fixed Peg Dynamics

Both the Saudi Riyal and UAE Dirham are officially pegged to the US Dollar. Consequently, whenever the USD strengthens or weakens against the Pakistani Rupee in the interbank or open market, SAR and AED rates move in direct tandem.

💶 Euro (EUR) Market Trends

Unlike regional Gulf currencies, the Euro floats independently on global forex markets. Economic data releases from the European Central Bank (ECB) heavily dictate its daily parity against the PKR.
