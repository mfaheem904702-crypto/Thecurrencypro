Two moving parts behind EUR to PKR

Unlike the dirham and riyal, the euro is a floating currency. Its price in rupees combines the euro-dollar rate with the dollar-rupee rate.

EUR to PKR≈EUR to USD×USD to PKR

Anything that lifts the euro against the dollar, such as European Central Bank decisions or stronger eurozone data, can raise EUR to PKR even if the rupee is steady. To follow the rupee side, see USD to PKR.

Who uses the euro rate in Pakistan

  • Students and workers abroad paying fees or sending savings home.
  • Families receiving transfers from relatives in Europe.
  • Exporters and importers trading with European buyers and suppliers.
  • Travellers budgeting for a trip to the eurozone.

Frequently asked questions

What is 1 EUR to PKR today?

The figure at the top of this page is the latest reference rate we fetched for one euro in Pakistani rupees. Banks and exchange companies quote their own rates, so confirm before you transact.

Why does EUR to PKR move more than AED or SAR?

The euro floats against the dollar, while the dirham and riyal are pegged to it. EUR to PKR therefore moves with both the euro-dollar rate and the dollar-rupee rate.

How is EUR to PKR calculated?

Roughly as EUR to USD multiplied by USD to PKR. If the euro gains on the dollar or the dollar gains on the rupee, the euro costs more rupees.

Who needs the euro rate in Pakistan?

Pakistanis studying, working or travelling in euro countries, families receiving money from Europe, and businesses paying European suppliers or selling to the EU.

Is the rate here the same as the exchange company or bank?

Usually not. This is a reference rate. Dealers add a margin and may charge fees, so compare the final rupee amount.

How often is the rate updated?
