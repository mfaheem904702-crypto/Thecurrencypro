Planning a Realistic International Travel Budget

Most travel budget surprises come from underestimating daily incidental spending — food, local transport, small purchases — rather than the big-ticket items like flights and hotels, which are usually booked and known in advance. Building a daily estimate that's slightly generous, then multiplying it by trip length, tends to give a more realistic total than assuming the bare minimum.

Why the USD Conversion Uses a Live Rate

The Rupee-to-Dollar rate moves daily, sometimes by a meaningful amount over the course of a multi-week trip-planning period. This calculator pulls the current live exchange rate each time you load the page, rather than relying on a fixed number that would quickly become outdated — so the USD equivalent shown reflects today's actual market rate, not a rate from whenever the page was last updated.

Costs This Calculator Doesn't Include

  • Visa fees, which vary significantly by destination country
  • Travel insurance
  • Airport transfers and local intercity transport beyond daily spending money
  • Currency exchange margins — banks and exchange companies typically don't offer the exact interbank rate shown here

Building in a buffer of 10-15% on top of this calculator's total is a reasonable practice for unexpected costs that come up on most trips.
