📊 USD to PKR History (2000 – Present)

The USD to PKR exchange rate is one of the most important financial indicators in Pakistan. It shows how strong or weak the Pakistani Rupee is compared to the US Dollar — directly affecting fuel prices, import costs, and daily expenses.

Below is a timeline of approximate annual average rates, based on published historical exchange-rate data:

20001 USD ≈ 52 PKR — Pakistan's economy was relatively stable with moderate inflation.
2008Rate jumped to roughly 70–79 PKR — driven by political instability and the global financial crisis.
2013Rate averaged around 101–107 PKR — a widening trade deficit kept pushing the rupee lower.
2018Rate crossed 120 PKR after a series of official devaluations amid mounting IMF bailout pressure.
2019–2021Rate rose sharply to roughly 163–179 PKR, then largely held in that range through 2021.
2022–2023A severe balance-of-payments crisis pushed the rate past 200 PKR, briefly spiking above 300 PKR in the open market in September 2023 before the State Bank's crackdown on the informal market pulled it back down.
2024–PresentRate has stayed comparatively stable, trading in roughly the 276–285 PKR band. See the live chart above for today's exact figure.
Figures above are rounded annual averages compiled from public exchange-rate history sources and may differ slightly from bank-quoted rates on any specific day. For the exact current rate, always refer to the live chart above.

💡 Major Reasons Behind Rupee Devaluation

1. Trade Deficit

Pakistan imports more goods than it exports, increasing demand for USD.

2. Inflation

High inflation reduces the purchasing power of PKR, making it weaker.

3. Foreign Debt

Loan repayments require dollars, increasing constant pressure on PKR.

4. Political Instability

Uncertainty reduces investor confidence and weakens the economy.

5. Low Foreign Reserves

Low reserves make it difficult to stabilize currency rates effectively.

🏠 Impact on Daily Life

The dollar rate directly affects the life of every Pakistani citizen. When dollar rises:

  • ⛽ Fuel prices increase
  • ⚡ Electricity bills rise
  • 📱 Imported goods become expensive
  • 🍞 Food prices increase
  • 🛒 Overall mehngai barh jati hai

This reduces purchasing power and increases financial pressure on households across Pakistan.

🏦 Open Market vs Interbank Rate

There are two main exchange rates in Pakistan:

  • Interbank Rate: Used by banks for official transactions — controlled by State Bank of Pakistan
  • Open Market Rate: Used by exchange companies — reflects real public demand
💱 Open market rate is usually slightly higher than interbank rate due to profit margins and demand pressure. The gap between them widens sharply during periods of currency stress, as happened in 2023.

🔮 Key Drivers to Watch Going Forward

Where USD to PKR heads next depends on several economic factors that are worth following rather than trying to predict a single number:

  • Exports growth (IT, textiles)
  • Inflation control
  • Foreign investment and reserve levels
  • Political stability
  • IMF program compliance and disbursements

If Pakistan sustains reforms on these fronts, the rupee has room to stabilize further. If reserves come under renewed pressure, the rate can move quickly — which is why checking the live chart is more useful than any fixed forecast.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

USD to PKR 2000 mein kitna tha?

2000 mein 1 USD ≈ 52 PKR ke qareeb tha. Us waqt Pakistan ki economy relatively stable thi.

Rupee itna weak kyun ho gaya?

Trade deficit, inflation, political instability, aur low foreign reserves — in sab wajuhat se Pakistani Rupee ka dollar ke muqable mein value gira hai, khaas kar 2018 aur 2022–23 ke crisis periods mein.

Kya dollar rate future mein stable hoga?

Yeh economic reforms, exports growth, aur IMF program par depend karta hai. Recent years mein rate 2022–23 ke muqable kaafi stable raha hai, lekin volatility hamesha possible hai — is liye live rate check karna behtar hai.
