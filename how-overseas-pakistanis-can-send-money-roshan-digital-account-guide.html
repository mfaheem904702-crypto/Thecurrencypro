📌 Overview: What is the Roshan Digital Account (RDA)?

The Roshan Digital Account (RDA) is a flagship initiative launched by the State Bank of Pakistan (SBP) in collaboration with major commercial banks. Designed specifically for Non-Resident Pakistanis (NRPs) and Pakistan Origin Card (POC) holders, RDA bridges the financial gap between overseas citizens and their home country.

Historically, opening a bank account in Pakistan from abroad required visiting a Pakistani consulate or embassy, physical verification, and extensive paperwork. With RDA, the entire process is conducted online from anywhere in the world within 48 hours.

💡 Key Advantage: Money transferred into a Roshan Digital Account can be fully repatriated back to your overseas bank account at any time without requiring prior approval from the State Bank of Pakistan.

1. Types of Roshan Digital Accounts Available

Applicants can choose between foreign currency accounts or local PKR accounts depending on their financial goals:

  • Foreign Currency Value Account (FCVA): Held in currencies such as USD, GBP, EUR, AED, SAR, and CAD. This option protects your foreign savings against exchange rate fluctuations while maintaining funds within Pakistan's banking framework.
  • NRP Rupee Value Account (NRVA): Held in Pakistani Rupees (PKR). Funds are converted at the prevailing interbank exchange rate upon deposit. It is ideal for local family support, utility bill payments, and investment in local equities or real estate.
  • Islamic vs. Conventional Options: All participating commercial banks (e.g., Meezan Bank, HBL, UBL, MCB, Allied Bank) offer both Shariah-compliant Islamic accounts and conventional accounts.

3. Eligibility & Required Documents

To open an RDA online, applicants must prepare clear scanned copies of the following documents before starting the application:

  1. Proof of Identity: Valid NICOP, CNIC, or Pakistan Origin Card (POC).
  2. Passport: First two pages of your valid Pakistani or foreign passport.
  3. Proof of Non-Resident Status: Valid foreign residence visa, green card, work permit, or passport entry/exit stamps.
  4. Proof of Profession & Income: Recent salary slip, employment letter, business registration, or foreign bank statement.
  5. Digital Live Selfie & Signature: Web-camera photograph captured live during registration along with a scanned specimen signature on plain white paper.

4. Investment Avenues: Naya Pakistan Certificates & Stock Market

RDA serves as a secure investment gateway for overseas wealth creation:

  • Naya Pakistan Certificates (NPCs): Sovereign debt securities issued by the Government of Pakistan offering competitive profit rates across foreign currencies (USD, GBP, EUR) and PKR. NPC profits are tax-free at source for Non-Resident Pakistanis.
  • Pakistan Stock Exchange (PSX): Trade directly on PSX listed companies through your bank portal.
  • Real Estate & DHA Projects: Finance commercial and residential property investments directly through authorized banking channels.

5. Step-by-Step Guide: How to Open & Remit Money

  1. Select Partner Bank: Choose an SBP-approved bank (e.g., HBL, Meezan Bank, UBL, MCB, ABL, Bank Alfalah).
  2. Complete Application Form: Fill out personal, employment, and FATCA/CRS tax details on the bank's official RDA portal.
  3. Upload Documents: Attach scanned files and complete live selfie photo verification.
  4. Account Activation: The bank validates credentials within 2 working days and issues IBAN & SWIFT details via email.
  5. Initiate Foreign Wire: Send funds directly from your overseas bank via international wire transfer using your RDA IBAN.
