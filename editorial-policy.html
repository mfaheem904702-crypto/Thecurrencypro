Editorial Standards & Policy

Learn how we maintain editorial integrity and research transparency across our platform.

At TheCurrencyPro, we strive to deliver precise, transparent, and accurate exchange rate data and financial research tailored for Pakistan and global markets.

1. Independent Financial Reporting

Our market insights, articles, and exchange rate guides are written and reviewed independently by experienced finance writers led by Muhammad Faheem. We do not accept payment to favor specific currencies, financial products, or institutions.

2. Data Verification & Sources

All exchange rates displayed on our portal are fetched via verified financial APIs (such as Open Exchange Rates API) and double-checked against the State Bank of Pakistan (SBP) interbank daily releases and Sarafa Market indicators.

3. Human Editorial Review

Every piece of educational content and economic forecast is subjected to human review before publication to ensure clarity, accuracy, and relevance.
