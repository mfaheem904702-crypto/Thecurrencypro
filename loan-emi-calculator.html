How Your Monthly Installment Is Actually Calculated

An EMI (Equal Monthly Installment) is calculated so that the same fixed payment covers both interest and gradually reduces the principal over the loan's tenure, using what's called the reducing-balance method. In early months, more of your payment goes toward interest; in later months, more goes toward the principal — even though the total payment amount stays the same throughout.

Why Tenure Length Matters More Than Most Borrowers Expect

Stretching a loan over a longer tenure lowers the monthly payment, which understandably appeals to borrowers focused on monthly affordability. But it also means paying interest for a longer period, which can substantially increase the total amount repaid over the life of the loan — sometimes by a large margin compared to a shorter tenure, even at the same interest rate. This calculator's "Total Interest" figure makes that trade-off visible rather than hidden in the monthly number alone.

Fixed vs. Variable Rate Considerations in Pakistan

Bank loan and mortgage rates in Pakistan are often linked to KIBOR (Karachi Interbank Offered Rate) plus a spread, meaning your actual rate can change over the loan's life if it's a variable-rate product. This calculator assumes a fixed rate for simplicity — for a variable-rate loan, treat the result as a snapshot at today's rate rather than a guaranteed figure for the full tenure.

A Practical Check Before Signing

Beyond the EMI itself, ask your bank about processing fees, early repayment penalties, and whether the rate is fixed or tied to KIBOR — these can meaningfully change the real cost of a loan beyond what a simple EMI calculation shows.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is EMI calculated? +
EMI uses the loan amount, monthly interest rate, and number of installments in a standard reducing-balance formula, so payments stay fixed while the interest/principal split shifts over time.
Why is total interest so much higher on longer tenures? +
A longer tenure lowers the monthly payment but extends the period interest accrues over, often significantly increasing total interest paid across the loan's life.
