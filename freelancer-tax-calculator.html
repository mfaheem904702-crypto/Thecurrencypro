How Freelancer Withholding Tax Works in Pakistan

When a freelancer or IT exporter in Pakistan receives payment from abroad — whether through Payoneer, Wise, a direct bank wire, or a marketplace like Upwork or Fiverr routed through a bank — the receiving bank deducts a small withholding tax at the point of conversion, under Section 154A of the Income Tax Ordinance covering IT and IT-enabled export income.

The rate applied depends on registration status with the Pakistan Software Export Board (PSEB):

  • 0.25% rate: Applies to freelancers and companies registered with PSEB as IT or IT-enabled services (ITeS) exporters.
  • 1% rate: Applies to general service exporters or freelancers who have not registered with PSEB.

This withholding tax is typically treated as a final tax on that export income for many freelancers, meaning no further income tax is usually due on that specific foreign-source freelance income beyond the amount already withheld — though overall tax obligations still depend on a freelancer's complete financial picture, including any other income sources.

Is PSEB Registration Worth It?

For a freelancer earning a modest, occasional amount from abroad, the tax rate difference (0.25% vs 1%) may only amount to a few thousand rupees a month. But for someone earning consistently — say $2,000-3,000 monthly — that gap compounds into a meaningful annual saving, and PSEB registration also opens access to other export facilitation schemes. Registration is done directly through PSEB's portal and generally requires basic business or freelancer documentation.

A Note on Accuracy

This calculator gives an estimate based on the withholding tax rate alone. Actual bank deductions can vary slightly depending on the specific payment channel and any bank service charges applied separately from the tax. For an exact figure, check your bank or payment provider's transaction breakdown, or consult a tax advisor for your specific situation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the withholding tax rate for freelancers in Pakistan? +
PSEB-registered IT exporters pay a reduced 0.25% withholding tax. Non-registered freelancers or general service exporters are typically subject to a 1% rate.
Do freelancers in Pakistan need to register with PSEB? +
It isn't mandatory, but PSEB registration unlocks the reduced 0.25% tax rate on IT and IT-enabled export income along with other export facilitation benefits.
