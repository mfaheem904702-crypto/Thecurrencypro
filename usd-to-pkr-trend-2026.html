📊 USD to PKR Trend in 2026

The USD to PKR exchange rate is one of the most important economic indicators in Pakistan. As of late July 2026, the interbank rate has been trading around Rs. 278 — relatively stable compared to the sharp depreciation seen in 2022–2023, but still under structural pressure from a widening trade gap.

Pakistan's FY2025–26 trade deficit widened to $39.5 billion, up roughly 21.6% year-on-year, as exports fell nearly 6% to $30.13 billion while imports rose about 7.9% to $69.6 billion. This growing gap between what Pakistan earns from exports and what it spends on imports remains the single biggest structural driver of pressure on the Rupee.

📈 2026 Trend: USD/PKR broadly range-bound in the high-Rs. 270s, with the trade deficit — not runaway inflation — the dominant pressure point this year.
Source: Pakistan Bureau of Statistics (PBS), SBP, July 2026

💡 Why USD to PKR Rate Increases?

There are several key reasons why the dollar rate faces upward pressure in Pakistan:

  • High Imports: FY26 imports rose to $69.6 billion, driven partly by duty relaxations and higher petroleum prices
  • Falling Exports: FY26 exports dropped to $30.1 billion, missing the government's $35.3 billion target by $5.2 billion
  • Inflation: CPI inflation was around 11.1% year-on-year in June 2026
  • Foreign Debt: Large external debt repayments (around $9 billion in Q4 FY26 alone) add to dollar demand
  • Lower Policy Rate: SBP's policy rate falling to 11.5% (from a 22% peak in 2024) has also boosted car and machinery imports

When these factors combine, demand for USD outpaces supply, which keeps the exchange rate under pressure.

Source: PBS, SBP, Business Recorder (July 2026)

🌍 Global Factors Affecting USD

Local factors aren't the only drivers — global conditions also significantly affect the USD to PKR rate:

  • US Federal Reserve interest rate decisions
  • Global oil price swings — a spike from late February to late June 2026, linked to the US-Iran conflict, directly pushed up Pakistan's import bill
  • International trade policy shifts
  • Regional geopolitical tensions in the Middle East and Europe, which analysts have also linked to Pakistan's export slowdown

Higher global oil prices mean Pakistan needs more dollars to cover its energy import bill — a direct channel of pressure on PKR.

Source: AKD Securities commentary via Business Recorder, July 2026

🏠 Impact on Daily Life in Pakistan

Currency and trade pressures have a direct impact on everyday life:

  • ⛽ Petrol and energy prices track global oil costs and PKR value
  • ⚡ Electricity bills remain sensitive to imported fuel costs
  • 🍞 Food and grocery prices contribute heavily to the ~11% CPI reading
  • 📱 Mobile phones, electronics, and vehicles — mostly imported — stay price-sensitive to USD/PKR moves
  • 🛒 Overall cost of living remains elevated versus pre-2022 levels

A weaker Rupee and a wider trade gap both work in the same direction: reducing the purchasing power of ordinary households.

💼 Impact on Freelancers & Online Earners

A higher dollar rate does benefit freelancers in Pakistan. People earning in USD receive more PKR when converting their income at the current interbank rate of around Rs. 278.

💰 Example: At roughly Rs. 278/USD, a $100 payment converts to about Rs. 27,800 before bank/platform fees — noticeably more than it would have at 2021-era rates near Rs. 160–170.

This is one reason Pakistani freelancers and remote workers track USD to PKR rates closely for income planning, alongside Pakistan's growing IT and freelance export earnings.

🏦 Interbank vs Open Market Rate

There are two main types of dollar rates in Pakistan:

  • Interbank Rate: Used by banks — regulated, currently around Rs. 278
  • Open Market Rate: Used by exchange shops — retail, typically Rs. 1–3 higher
The open market rate is usually slightly higher than the interbank rate due to dealer margins and cash-handling costs.

Here's how Pakistan's trade position — the key driver behind USD/PKR pressure — evolved through fiscal year 2025–26 (July 2025–June 2026):

📅 Jul – May FY26

📅 Full FY26

Source: Pakistan Bureau of Statistics (PBS), FY2025-26 data

🔮 Future Prediction of USD to PKR

Experts believe the near-term path for PKR depends on several factors:

  • Whether exports can recover toward the FY27 target of $32.8 billion
  • Import growth — analysts already expect FY27 imports to cross $70 billion
  • Continued IMF programme compliance, after Pakistan missed its FY26 tax collection target by around $3.5 billion
  • Global oil prices and Middle East geopolitical stability

The government has set an FY27 trade deficit target of around $37 billion, only slightly narrower than FY26's $39.5 billion — suggesting policymakers expect continued, if moderating, pressure on the currency.

Source: Profit/Pakistan Today, July 2026

📌 Final Conclusion

The USD to PKR trend in 2026 reflects Pakistan's ongoing structural trade imbalance more than runaway inflation — the FY26 trade deficit widened to $39.5 billion even as inflation cooled to around 11%. Understanding the reasons behind these changes can help individuals and businesses make smarter decisions.

By tracking rates, analyzing trends, and staying informed, you can plan your finances more effectively around currency movements.
