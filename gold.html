How the gold price in Pakistan is built

Global price

Gold trades in US dollars per troy ounce on international markets.

Convert to rupees

The ounce price is multiplied by the USD to PKR rate.

Convert the unit

One troy ounce is 31.1035 g, and one tola is 11.6638 g.

Add local costs

Premium, making charges, duties and taxes sit on top.

24K per tola ≈ (gold price per ounce in USD × USD to PKR ÷ 31.1035) × 11.6638

Because gold is imported and paid for in dollars, a weaker rupee can raise the local price even when the global price does not move. Check the current USD to PKR rate alongside the gold price.

Why your jeweller's rate differs from an online estimate

  • Local premium: demand, supply and market liquidity add a margin over the international price.
  • Making charges and wastage: jewellery is priced above the metal value.
  • Duties and taxes: these are not part of a simple price formula.
  • Timing: sarafa rates are updated through the day while a reference rate may be older.

Checklist before you buy gold

  • Ask for the day's 24K rate and the karat of the item, and get both in writing on the invoice.
  • Ask how making charges are calculated (per gram or a percentage) and compare two or three shops.
  • Check the weight on a calibrated scale and ask what is deducted if you sell back.
  • Keep the receipt; it supports resale and any future dispute.

Frequently asked questions

How many grams are in one tola?

One tola is 11.6638 grams, the standard unit in Pakistani sarafa markets. A troy ounce is 31.1035 grams, or about 2.67 tola.

What is the difference between 24K and 22K gold?

24K is about 99.9% pure gold and is used for bars and coins. 22K is about 91.6% gold mixed with other metals for strength, and is the usual standard for traditional jewellery.

How is the gold price in Pakistan calculated?

Roughly: the global price per ounce in US dollars, multiplied by the USD to PKR rate, divided by 31.1035 for grams, then multiplied by 11.6638 for a tola. Local premium, making charges, duties and taxes are added on top.

Why does the gold rate rise when the dollar rate rises?

Gold is priced in dollars internationally, so a weaker rupee means more rupees per ounce. The local price can rise even if the global price is unchanged.

Why is the sarafa rate different from this estimate?

The estimate uses the international price and a reference exchange rate. Sarafa rates include local demand, premium and timing, and jewellery adds making charges.

Which purity is best for investment?

Many buyers choose 24K bars or coins for investment because they carry no alloy and lower making costs, while 22K is typically bought as jewellery. Compare charges and buy-back terms before deciding.
