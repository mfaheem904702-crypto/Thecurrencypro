Content Update & Verification Policy

Our standards for keeping financial data, guides, and tax calculators fully updated and accurate.

Check Live Dollar & Currency Market Trends

1. Continuous Technical Rate Updates

All automated currency rate displays, ticker boards, and exchange converters run on automated API triggers that refresh data dynamically across trading sessions. System health is monitored constantly to prevent stale rate display.

2. Weekly Editorial Content Audits

Financial laws, tax brackets, and macroeconomic trends change frequently. We review our written currency analysis and market guides weekly to ensure all referenced statistics, historical comparisons, and market projections remain current and accurate.

3. Annual Financial Tool Audits

Interactive tools—such as our Salary Tax Calculator, Freelancer Tax Tools, and Zakat Calculators—are comprehensively reviewed whenever new national fiscal policies or annual budget tax revisions are announced by tax authorities.

4. How We Mark Modified Content

To ensure reader transparency, all major articles and guides explicitly show the "Last Updated" date at the top of the page. Minor grammatical fixes do not trigger timestamp changes, but substantive factual updates always do.

5. Reader Corrections & Feedback

If you notice a discrepancy or outdated figure on any page, please inform us immediately via our Contact Page or review our Corrections Policy.
