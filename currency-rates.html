How to read these rates

Each row shows how many rupees one unit of the currency buys, based on a reference rate. The last column shows what Rs. 10,000 would convert to, which is useful for sending or receiving money.

The rate you actually get can differ. Banks use the interbank rate as a base, exchange companies add a margin for walk-in customers, and platforms add their own markups. Compare the final rupee amount before you transact. Read more in interbank vs open market.

Frequently asked questions

What is the exchange rate of the dollar in Pakistan today?

Open the USD to PKR page for the latest reference rate and converter. The table here shows the dollar and other major currencies against the rupee.

Why are the rates here different from my exchange company?

The table shows reference rates. Exchange companies and banks add their own margin and fees, and the open market rate can differ from the interbank rate.

Which currencies have their own page?

USD, EUR, GBP, AED, SAR, QAR, OMR, CAD, AUD and CNY. Click a currency name in the table to open its converter and guide.

How often does the table update?

It fetches the latest figures from open.er-api.com each time the page loads. The provider updates on its own schedule, shown as Source update, so treat the numbers as reference rates.

What is the difference between interbank and open market rates?

The interbank rate is used by banks trading with each other. The open market rate is what exchange companies quote to walk-in customers and includes their margin.
